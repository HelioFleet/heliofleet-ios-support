# HelioFleet for iOS – Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 16 October 2026_

HelioFleet for iOS ("the app") is a utility for receiving photos, videos and files from a
computer. That computer can also read basic device status and change screen brightness. This policy
explains what the app does with information. In short: **the app does not collect,
store remotely, share or sell any personal data.**

## Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting
services and no third-party SDKs. It never sends data to HelioFleet or to anyone else.

## How the app works

- The app runs a small web server **on your device**, only while the app is open. A
  computer connected by USB, or on the same Wi-Fi network, can connect to it.
- Connections over Wi-Fi need a PIN that is shown only inside the app.
- The app never connects out to the internet or to other devices on its own.

## Files you send to the device

- **Photos and videos** you send are saved to your Photos library. The app asks for
  **add-only** access. It can add items but cannot see, read or delete anything already
  in your library.
- **Other files** you send are saved in the app's own folder, which you can see in the
  Files app under "On My iPhone › HelioFleet". You can delete them there at any time, or
  by deleting the app.
- These files stay on your device. The app does not upload them anywhere.

## Device information

When a connected computer asks, the app reports basic device status to that computer
only:

- Model and iOS version
- Battery level, temperature level and Low Power Mode
- Free storage
- Screen brightness and size
- Language and time zone
- Accessibility settings

This information is not stored or sent anywhere else. The app also shows it on its own
screen.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Photos (add only) | To save photos and videos you send to the device |

You can change this permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
